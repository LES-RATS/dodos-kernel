# Classification of ARM64 (A64), from Arm's AARCHMRS (D-125).
# One line per encoding: KIND NAME CLASS [REASON]. `classify items aarch64`
# lists the encodings and the domain (see src/aarch64.rs). The class of a
# register governs its writes; its reads are allowed wherever it is
# `kernel` or `gate`. The kernel runs at EL2 where EL2 exists, with the EL2
# forms of the EL1 registers (kernel.md, ARM64), else at EL1.

why UNUSED kernel-interface.md#architectures "*unused* (refused)" -- not used by the kernel
why EL3 kernel-interface.md#what-it-costs-and-what-it-does-not-cover "Not covered: ... firmware (SMM, EL3, machine mode)" and kernel-interface.md#architectures "*unreachable* (traps or is undefined at the kernel's level; refused anyway)"
why VHE-ALIAS kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "(and their EL2 forms when the kernel runs at EL2, as it does where EL2 exists ...)" -- the _EL12 and _EL02 forms reach the EL1 and EL0 registers of a guest regime the kernel does not run: unused
why TRANSLATION kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "Forbidden outside the gate: writes to `TTBR0_EL1`, `TTBR1_EL1`, `TCR_EL1`, `TCR2_EL1`, `SCTLR_EL1`, `MAIR_EL1`, `VBAR_EL1`, `POR_EL1`, `PIR_EL1`, `PIRE0_EL1` ... (and their EL2 forms ...)" and kernel-interface.md#the-rules "G1. ... the only code that loads a translation root and that changes the state that controls translation"
why WALKER kernel-interface.md#the-gate-over-page-tables "\"Translation\" covers the CPU page tables and TLBs under every tag, the IOMMU tables X writes, and any other walker X could configure." -- a register with a table or buffer base (field BADDR or TTBA)
why START kernel-interface.md#the-gate-over-page-tables "(c) no other instruction can redefine translation or reach memory around it (the build-time scan and the configuration locked at start-up)" and kernel-interface.md#the-rules "G11. ... refuses any instance outside the gate and the start-up code that runs before the protection is on"
why STAGE2 kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "the EL2 translation registers unless the image declares the hardening monitor"
why STAGE2-USE kernel-interface.md#the-gate-over-page-tables "A faulty kernel is bounded to its own memory without two-stage translation (D-117)." -- stage-2 maintenance is not used
why VECTOR kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "the vector-length registers `ZCR_EL1` and `SMCR_EL1` (and their EL2 forms), which keep one length per kind (D-118), and the `MSR` immediates that change the streaming vector state, `SVCRSM`, `SVCRZA` and `SVCRSMZA` (the forms `SMSTART` and `SMSTOP`), for the same reason"
why SGI kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "the interrupt generation registers `ICC_SGI0R_EL1`, `ICC_SGI1R_EL1` and `ICC_ASGI1R_EL1` ...: a fence signal and an ordinary interrupt to another core are the same instruction with different operands, so the scan forbids every write and the gate makes them, checking G10"
why GIC-DIST kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "An SPI is routed to a core by `GICD_IROUTER<n>` of the distributor ...; both are MMIO regions mapped only in the gate view of the holder of the unit role" -- GICv5 makes routing, enabling, priority and pending state instructions (Linux v7.3, drivers/irqchip/irq-gic-v5.c)
why DEBUG-REGS kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "the debug registers `DBGBCR<n>_EL1`, `DBGBVR<n>_EL1`, `DBGWCR<n>_EL1`, `DBGWVR<n>_EL1` and `MDSCR_EL1`, writable at EL1 ..., which could arm a watchpoint on another node's memory"
why DEBUG-STATE kernel-interface.md#what-it-costs-and-what-it-does-not-cover "on ARM64 ... the writes of the debug and vector-length registers" -- the other self-hosted debug registers
why GCS kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`GCSCR_EL1`, `GCSPR_EL1` (and their EL2 forms ...)" and "Page tables typed as Guarded Control Stack pages, written by `GCSSTR`" -- every GCS instruction moves GCSPR or stores to a GCS page
why FIRMWARE kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`SMC` and `HVC`" and kernel-interface.md#the-rules "Firmware calls are made only by the gate"
why DC-ISW kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`DC ISW`"
why AROUND kernel-interface.md#the-gate-over-page-tables "(c) no other instruction can redefine translation or reach memory around it" -- cache maintenance by physical address
why BROADCAST kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`INVLPGB` and `TLBSYNC` (AMD's broadcast invalidation, which reaches other cores' TLBs)" -- the same reason applied to the shareable (IS, OS) TLBI and PLBI forms; listed for the owners
why IMPDEF kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "implementation-defined system registers" -- and every system encoding the release does not name
why Q-RAS question -- an error record selected by ERRSELR_EL1 may be shared by cores of several nodes (code 7 of the crash record reads them): may any kernel clear or configure it, or only the gate?
why Q-CDDI question -- GIC CDDI deactivates an interrupt named by its ID (Linux v7.3 calls it at the end of every interrupt): kernel.md says the GICv3 CPU interface acts on the calling core only; does that hold for GICv5's CDDI, or must it be gate?
