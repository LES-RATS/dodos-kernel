# Classification of RISC-V (RV64), from the RISC-V unified database (D-125).
# One line per encoding: KIND NAME CLASS [REASON]. `classify items riscv64`
# lists the encodings and the domain (see src/riscv64.rs). The class of a
# CSR governs every CSR instruction that may write it; its pure reads are
# allowed wherever it is `kernel` or `gate`.

why UNUSED kernel-interface.md#architectures "*unused* (refused)" -- not used by the kernel
why RV32 kernel-interface.md#architectures "*unreachable* (traps or is undefined at the kernel's level; refused anyway)" -- an RV32-only CSR; the kernel is RV64
why MACHINE kernel-interface.md#what-it-costs-and-what-it-does-not-cover "Not covered: ... firmware (SMM, EL3, machine mode)" and kernel-interface.md#architectures "*unreachable* (traps or is undefined at the kernel's level; refused anyway)" -- machine mode
why DEBUG-MODE kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "The trigger registers of the debug specification (`tselect`, `tdata1` to `tdata3`) are reachable only from machine mode and debug mode under Sdtrig" -- debug mode only
why SATP kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "Forbidden outside the gate (scanned at every 2-byte offset): writes to `satp`"
why STVEC kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "Forbidden outside the gate ...: writes to ... `stvec`"
why SSP kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "Forbidden outside the gate ...: writes to ... `ssp`"
why SENVCFG kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "Forbidden outside the gate ...: writes to ... `senvcfg`"
why HYPERVISOR kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "Forbidden outside the gate ...: writes to ... the hypervisor CSRs"
why ECALL kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`ECALL`" and kernel-interface.md#the-rules "Firmware calls are made only by the gate"
why SHADOW kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "the shadow-stack stores"
why CBO-INVAL kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`CBO.INVAL`"
why HYPERVISOR-INSN kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "the hypervisor fences and loads"
