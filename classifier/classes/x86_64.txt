# Classification of x86-64, from Intel XED's instruction data (D-125).
# One line per encoding: KIND NAME CLASS [REASON]. `classify items x86_64`
# lists the encodings and the domain (see src/x86_64.rs).
#
# Reasons: the sentence of the contract (kernel-interface.md) or of
# kernel.md ("The write gate: mechanisms") that justifies the class.

why UNUSED kernel-interface.md#architectures "Every encoding of the pinned release is classified once, as *gate* (...), *kernel* (used by the kernel, harmless to other nodes), *unreachable* (traps or is undefined at the kernel's level; refused anyway) or *unused* (refused)" -- not used by the kernel
why UNREACHABLE kernel-interface.md#architectures "*unreachable* (traps or is undefined at the kernel's level; refused anyway)" -- invalid in 64-bit mode
why CR kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "Instructions forbidden outside the gate and the start-up code ...: writes to CR0, CR3, CR4 and the debug registers, among them `LMSW` and `CLTS`, which write CR0 by other opcodes"
why MSR kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`WRMSR`, `WRMSRNS`, `WRMSRLIST` and the MSR forms with an immediate index (XED's `msr-imm`)"
why XSTATE kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`XSETBV` (it sets XCR0, the vector state features: the counterpart of `ZCR_EL1` under D-118) and `XRSTORS`"
why TABLES kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`LGDT`, `LIDT`, `LLDT`, `LTR`"
why INVD kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`INVD`"
why BROADCAST kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`INVLPGB` and `TLBSYNC` (AMD's broadcast invalidation, which reaches other cores' TLBs)"
why VIRT kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "the VMX and SVM instructions (unless the image declares the hardening monitor)"
why PORTS kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`IN`, `OUT` and their string forms" and kernel-interface.md#io-ports "the gate executes one `in` or `out` of that width at that port"
why STRING-IO kernel-interface.md#io-ports "No string I/O, no batch, no exclusive ranges (design choice). No device of version 1 needs string I/O."
why SHADOW kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "the shadow-stack management instructions, `SETSSBSY`, `CLRSSBSY`, `WRUSSD`, `WRUSSQ` and `WRSSQ` outside the gate's mechanism"
why FRED kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`ERETS`, `ERETU` and `LKGS` (FRED, deferred, refused all the same)"
why IWKEY kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`LOADIWKEY`"
why SEAM kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "the SEAM and TDX calls"
why SNP kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "the SEV-SNP instructions (`PVALIDATE`, `RMPADJUST`, `RMPUPDATE`, `PSMASH`)"
why HRESET kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`HRESET`"
why ENCLAVE kernel.md#the-write-gate-mechanisms "`ENCLS`, `PCONFIG`"
why UINTR kernel-interface.md#user-interrupts-a-placed-hook "Processors that deliver an interrupt straight to a user thread (Intel's user interrupts and their equivalents) are not used in version 1"
why FIRMWARE kernel-interface.md#the-rules "G10. ... the kernel never calls firmware runtime services (UEFI's or any other)" -- SMM, measured launch
